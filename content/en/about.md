Ancha Xu is a Professor of Statistics and Ph.D. supervisor at Zhejiang Gongshang University. His research interests include degradation data analysis and modeling, Bayesian online learning, objective Bayesian methods, and lifetime data analysis. He has published more than 60 SCI papers as first or corresponding author in leading reliability and statistics journals, including *Naval Research Logistics*, *Scandinavian Journal of Statistics*, *Journal of Quality Technology*, *European Journal of Operational Research*, *IEEE Transactions on Reliability*, and *IISE Transactions*, with 6 ESI Highly Cited Papers. He has served as principal investigator on more than ten projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and by provincial agencies. His honors include the Zhejiang Provincial Natural Science Award, the Fujian Provincial Natural Science Award, and the National Award for Statistical Science and Technology Progress. He was selected for Zhejiang’s Academician–Expert Mentoring Program for Young Talent and the Zhejiang High-Level Talent Special Support Program (Young Talent in Education). He currently serves as Vice Chair of the Reliability Society of the Operations Research Society of China, standing council member of the Reliability Engineering Society and the Big Data Statistics Society of the Chinese Association for Applied Statistics, and Associate Editor of *Statistical Theory and Related Fields*.
